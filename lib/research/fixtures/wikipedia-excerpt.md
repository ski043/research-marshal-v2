The **lithium iron phosphate battery** (**LiFePO  
4 battery**) or **LFP battery** (_lithium ferrophosphate_) is a type of [lithium-ion battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium-ion_battery "Lithium-ion battery") using [lithium iron phosphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate "Lithium iron phosphate") (LiFePO  
4) as the [cathode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathode "Cathode") material, and a [graphitic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphitic "Graphitic") [carbon electrode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_electrode "Carbon electrode") with a metallic backing as the [anode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anode "Anode"). Because of their low cost, high safety, low toxicity, long cycle life and other factors, LFP batteries are finding a number of roles in [vehicle use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_vehicle_battery "Electric vehicle battery"), utility-scale stationary applications, and [backup power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backup_power "Backup power").[\[8\]](#cite_note-8) As of September 2022, LFP type battery market share for [EVs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_vehicle "Electric vehicle") reached 31%, and of that, 68% were from EV makers [Tesla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla,_Inc. "Tesla, Inc.") and [BYD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BYD_Auto "BYD Auto") alone.[\[9\]](#cite_note-9)
